::algorithm-facts{name="blake256"}
::

The BLAKE that lost the SHA-3 competition to Keccak and then got a second life in Decred, which hashes its blocks, transactions and addresses with it. Not BLAKE2, not BLAKE3, and the digests have nothing in common.

```ts
create("blake256").hash("").digest;
// "716f6e863f744b9ac22c97ec7b76ea5f5908bc5b2f67c61510bfc4751384ea7a"
```

14 rounds, as the final submission specified. No HMAC mode.
