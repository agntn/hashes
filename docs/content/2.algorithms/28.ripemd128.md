---
title: RIPEMD-128
description: The four-round RIPEMD from 1996 with a 16-byte digest. Too short for new designs and it has HMAC
---

::algorithm-facts{name="ripemd128"}
::

[RIPEMD-160](/algorithms/ripemd160) cut down. Two parallel lines like its big sibling, but four rounds instead of five, four words instead of five, and no rotation by 10. Dobbertin, Bosselaers and Preneel published both in 1996, this one as a replacement for the original RIPEMD.

```ts
create("ripemd128").hash("abc").digest; // "c14a12199c66e4ba84636b0f69144c77"
```

Is it broken? Not like [MD5](/algorithms/md5). But 16 bytes buy 64-bit collision resistance, and that's a budget a determined attacker can spend. So it sits under legacy. Old formats, old papers, and the odd puzzle that likes obscure hashes.

Need 32 bytes from the same lines? That's [RIPEMD-256](/algorithms/ripemd256), and it's not any stronger.
